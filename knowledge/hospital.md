## About Xplore Hospital
Xplore Hospital is a 250-bed multispeciality hospital in Chennai. We have been caring for families since 2008. The hospital is NABH accredited and has a 24x7 emergency department, ICU, modern operation theatres, an in-house pharmacy, laboratory and radiology services.

## Address, location and directions
Xplore Hospital is located at No. 42, Anna Salai, Teynampet, Chennai, Tamil Nadu 600018. The nearest metro station is Teynampet Metro, a 3-minute walk from the hospital. We are opposite the Teynampet bus stop. Free visitor parking is available in the basement for cars and two-wheelers.

## Contact numbers and email
For appointments and general enquiries call our front desk at +91 44 4000 1234 (8 AM to 8 PM, all days). For emergencies and ambulance call +91 44 4000 1108, available 24 hours. WhatsApp appointments: +91 98400 12345. Email: care@xplorehospital.example.

## Hospital timings and OPD hours
Outpatient (OPD) consultations run Monday to Saturday from 8:00 AM to 8:00 PM. On Sundays the OPD is open from 9:00 AM to 1:00 PM for general medicine and paediatrics only. The emergency department, pharmacy and laboratory are open 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, including public holidays.

## Visiting hours for inpatients
Visiting hours for admitted patients are 11:00 AM to 1:00 PM and 5:00 PM to 7:00 PM every day. Only two visitors are allowed per patient at a time. Children under 12 are not allowed inside the ICU. ICU visiting is allowed for 15 minutes at 12 noon and 6 PM for one close relative.

## Emergency and ambulance services
Our emergency department is open 24x7 with emergency physicians, trauma care, a cath lab for heart attacks and a stroke team. For an ambulance call +91 44 4000 1108. In case of a medical emergency, do not wait to book an appointment — come directly to the emergency entrance on the Anna Salai side.

## Departments and services
Xplore Hospital offers the following departments: General Medicine, Cardiology (heart care), Orthopaedics (bones and joints), Paediatrics (child care), Obstetrics and Gynaecology (women's health and pregnancy care), Neurology, ENT, Dermatology (skin care), Dental care, Ophthalmology (eye care), Physiotherapy, Dietetics, and Radiology (X-ray, CT scan, MRI, ultrasound). We also have a 24x7 pharmacy and a NABL-accredited diagnostic laboratory.

## How to book an appointment
You can book an appointment in four ways: 1) Call the front desk at +91 44 4000 1234 between 8 AM and 8 PM. 2) Send a WhatsApp message to +91 98400 12345 with the patient name, age, department and preferred date. 3) Use the "Book Appointment" button on our website. 4) Walk in to the OPD registration counter on the ground floor. Online bookings get a confirmation SMS within 15 minutes. Please arrive 15 minutes early with a photo ID and any previous reports.

## Rescheduling and cancelling an appointment
To reschedule or cancel, call +91 44 4000 1234 or reply to your confirmation SMS at least 2 hours before your slot. There is no cancellation charge.

## First visit — what to bring
For your first visit please bring a government photo ID (Aadhaar, PAN, driving licence or passport), previous prescriptions and test reports, your insurance card if you plan to use insurance, and a list of medicines you are currently taking. Registration takes about 10 minutes and the one-time registration fee is ₹100.

## Insurance and cashless treatment
Xplore Hospital offers cashless treatment for inpatient admissions with these insurers and TPAs: Star Health, HDFC ERGO, ICICI Lombard, Niva Bupa, Care Health, Bajaj Allianz, New India Assurance, United India Insurance, Medi Assist, MD India and Paramount TPA. We also accept CGHS and the Tamil Nadu Chief Minister's Comprehensive Health Insurance Scheme (CMCHIS). Outpatient consultations are usually not covered by insurance and are paid directly.

## Insurance — documents and pre-authorisation process
For cashless admission, visit the insurance desk on the ground floor (open 8 AM to 8 PM) with your insurance card or policy copy, a photo ID, and the doctor's admission advice. The desk sends the pre-authorisation request to your insurer; approval usually takes 2 to 6 hours. For planned surgeries, please complete pre-authorisation at least 2 days in advance. For emergencies, the hospital starts treatment immediately and processes insurance in parallel.

## Payment methods and billing
We accept cash, all major debit and credit cards, UPI (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm) and net banking. EMI options are available for planned surgeries above ₹50,000 through partner banks. The billing counter is on the ground floor and is open 24 hours.

## Laboratory, scans and reports
Blood sample collection is open 24x7. Most blood test reports are ready the same day by 6 PM and are sent by SMS and WhatsApp. CT and MRI scans need a prior appointment; ultrasound and X-ray are available on walk-in. Home sample collection is available within 10 km for an extra ₹200; call +91 44 4000 1234 to book.

## Pharmacy
The in-house pharmacy is on the ground floor and is open 24 hours. Home delivery of medicines is available within 5 km for orders above ₹500.

## Facilities for patients and attendants
We have a cafeteria on the 1st floor (7 AM to 10 PM), free Wi-Fi, wheelchair assistance at the main entrance, a prayer room, ATMs on the ground floor and attendant beds in private rooms. Rooms available: general ward, twin sharing, single private room and deluxe suite.
